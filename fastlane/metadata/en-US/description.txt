Rotate is a simple journal for where you place your insulin pump and CGM sensor — and a nudge on when to move them next.

Rotating sites gives your skin time to recover. Rotate keeps your pump and sensor as two separate tracks, shows a color-coded body map of which areas you've used recently, and suggests a fresh spot each time — so you're not guessing or crowding the same patch of skin.

WHAT IT DOES
• Two independent tracks — pump and CGM, each with its own history and body map
• A recency heatmap: recently-used areas stand out, rested ones invite reuse
• Time-on and average wear per track, right on the home screen
• A quick new-placement flow with placement guidance
• Optional hand-off: after you log a placement, jump straight to Loop or the Dexcom app to activate and pair

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your journal lives on your device and syncs through your own private iCloud. No accounts, no third-party servers, no tracking, no ads.

Rotate is a journaling and reminder aid. It is not a medical device and does not provide dosing, diagnosis, or treatment advice — always follow your clinician and your device's instructions. Rotate is not affiliated with Insulet, Dexcom, or Loop.
