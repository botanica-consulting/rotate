What's new in 1.1.1

• Fix times after the fact — tap any journal entry to change when a site went on or came off.
• Add your own sites for spots the body figure doesn't cover. They rotate, collect recency heat, and can be included or excluded per track like any other area.
• Turn iCloud sync off. Sync stays on by default, but you can now keep your journal on one device only — and remove the copy already in your iCloud.
• Ask Siri how long your pump or sensor has been on, or start the next site hands-free.
• A lock-screen widget showing the hour count — tap it to start a new site.
• Clearer privacy wording: the app now says plainly that Apple holds the synced copy while sync is on, and it hides your journal in the app switcher.
